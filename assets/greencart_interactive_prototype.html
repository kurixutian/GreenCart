Estimation Methodology

In compliance with GreenCart data-integrity standards, environmental figures are displayed as documented estimates derived per completed paperless transaction:

  • Paper Saved (Sheets) 1.00 base sheet + (0.25 × distinct line items)
  • Carbon Reduction (g CO₂e) Paper Saved (sheets) × 3.50 g CO₂e/sheet

1-to-1 cardinality enforced between transactions and eco_impact.
